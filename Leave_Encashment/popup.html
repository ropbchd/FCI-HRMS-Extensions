📋 FCI Leave Encashment Assistant

Active on FCI HRMS Leave Encashment pages.

Step 1: Reads Office & Encashment from listing page, opens request detail.

Step 2: Routes request to assistant for eligibility check.

Step 3: Fills final approval remark with dynamic validation.

How to use:
1. Navigate to Leave Encashment listing page
2. Extension auto-opens the first pending request detail
3. Extension auto-clicks "Add Reviewer" → fills form, you click Add
4. After assistant reviews, extension fills final remark, you click Review
